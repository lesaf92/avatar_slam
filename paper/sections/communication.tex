\section{Medium-Aware Communication}
\label{sec:comm}

\subsection{Wire format}
Each landmark part is encoded in $16 + D$ bytes: position at 5~cm resolution, isotropic horizontal and
vertical uncertainty, footprint and part height, class, medium/modality flags, observation count,
and an optional $D$-dimensional int8 descriptor; packets carry a 10-byte header and a CRC-16
(docs/spec/wire\_format\_v0.md). \todo{justify resolutions vs.\ sensor noise.}
