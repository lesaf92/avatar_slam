\section{Problem Formulation}
\label{sec:problem}

\subsection{Agents, media, and frames}
A team $\mathcal{A}$ of agents operates in air, on ground, on the water surface, and underwater.
All frames are right-handed ENU and gravity-aligned; the vertical datum $z=0$ is the waterline
(docs/conventions.md). Agent $i$ estimates its trajectory in a local map frame $L_i$ whose
origin lies at its start position and whose $x$-axis follows its start heading. Because roll and
pitch are observable from gravity and every agent references the waterline (pressure sensor,
barometer, hull, or known quay height), local frames differ by a 4-DoF transform
$\Tf{i}{j} = (\mathbf{t}_{ij}, \theta_{ij}) \in \R^3 \times S^1$ with $t^z_{ij} \approx 0$.
Poses are $\mathbf{x}_k = (\p_k, \psi_k)$.

\subsection{Landmark parts and the coaxial model}
A structure $s$ spanning $[z^{\min}_s, z^{\max}_s]$ that crosses the waterline yields an
\emph{above} part $\lm^{+}_s$ and a \emph{below} part $\lm^{-}_s$: the centroids of the portions
visible to optical and to acoustic sensors, respectively. Both share the horizontal position but
not the height:
\begin{equation}
  \Pxy\,\lm^{+}_s = \Pxy\,\lm^{-}_s, \qquad \Pxy = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0\\ 0 & 1 & 0\end{bmatrix}.
  \label{eq:coaxial}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Communication model}
Each agent carries a subset of link types $\{\mathrm{RF}, \mathrm{AC}\}$. A packet of $B$ bytes
sent at share $\sigma$ of bit rate $\beta$ over distance $d$ arrives after
$8B/(\sigma\beta) + \tau_0 + d/c$ with probability $1 - p_{\mathrm{loss}}(d)$, provided both
endpoints are in a medium that supports the link (RF: $z \geq -\epsilon$; acoustic:
$z \leq \epsilon$). \todo{justify parameter ranges with modem/radio datasheets.}

\subsection{Problem statement}
Given each agent's own measurements and the packets it receives, estimate (i) its trajectory and
landmark parts in $L_i$ and (ii) the transforms $\Tf{i}{j}$ to its neighbors, such that all
agents can be expressed in a common team frame, without a central server, while respecting
each link's byte budget.
