\section{Cross-Medium Association}
\label{sec:association}

\subsection{Candidate gating}
Pairs of landmark parts are gated by medium (same medium: 3D residual; above/below: horizontal
residual only), semantic class, yaw-invariant footprint ratio, height ratio (same medium), and
descriptor cosine similarity when both carry open-vocabulary descriptors.

\subsection{Consistency graph and hypotheses}
Two candidate pairs are consistent if they use distinct landmarks and preserve horizontal
distance, and, if both are same-medium, vertical offset, within the sum of their uncertainty-scaled
gates. Hypotheses are greedy maximal cliques of this graph, in the spirit of
PCM~\cite{mangelson2018pcm}; each is refined by weighted 4-DoF Kabsch~\cite{umeyama1991} and
scored with a one-to-one MSAC score in which cross-medium inliers weigh $2/3$.
